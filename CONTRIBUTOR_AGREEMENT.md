# MADI3D Contributor and Source Access Agreement

Version 2.0 — 26 August 2026

This agreement applies to any person or organization that is granted access to
non-public MADI3D source code or submits material for inclusion in MADI3D.
Acceptance must be recorded in writing before private repository access is
granted or a contribution is accepted.

MADI3D uses a mixed licensing model. Most application source remains
proprietary, while selected scientific components identified in
`OpenSource/README.md` may be published under AGPL-3.0-only.

## 5. Confidential source access

Non-public MADI3D source code and other non-public project material made
available to you are confidential project material. Unless the project
maintainers authorize otherwise in writing, you must not publish, disclose,
redistribute, sublicense, upload, mirror, or otherwise provide that material to
any third party.

This confidentiality obligation does not apply to material that the MADI3D
project has expressly published or expressly designated for public release.
Open-source designation of one file or component does not make unrelated
private repository content public.

You may make only the copies reasonably necessary for the authorized MADI3D
work and must take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access.

Accepted contributions may remain in the proprietary source tree or may be
published as part of the open-source scientific scope. Your retained copyright
does not override confidentiality obligations for material that remains
non-public.

## 6. No obligation to accept, publish, or maintain contributions

Submission does not require the MADI3D project to accept, distribute, publish,
maintain, credit, or continue using a contribution. The project may modify or
remove an accepted contribution as the software evolves.

Designation for possible open-source publication does not require the project
to publish a contribution on a particular date or through a particular
distribution channel.

This agreement does not create employment, agency, partnership, joint venture,
or an entitlement to payment. Paid work, sponsored development, consulting,
and other compensated services require a separate written agreement.

Nothing in this agreement changes the license of third-party components used by

obligations before introducing them.

## 9. Survival

The licenses granted for submitted contributions and the confidentiality
obligations relating to non-public source survive termination of repository
access or the contributor relationship.
