Mesa / LLVM software OpenGL renderer bundled with Qt on Windows

The inspected PySide6 Essentials 6.9.3 opengl32sw.dll reports Mesa 11.2.2
and LLVM 3.6.2. Its SHA-256 is
1c162c98c06948b8359408eaf23a9d71ab37380351f9868a73bcc26b5e04e140.
It is a separate bundled native renderer, not covered only by Qt's LGPL text.

Qt build description: https://wiki.qt.io/MesaLlvmpipe
Qt upstream binaries: https://download.qt.io/development_releases/prebuilt/llvmpipe/windows/

The following original license texts/headers cover Mesa's MIT license,
Gallium/llvmpipe, Khronos headers, C11 thread support, LLVM's University of
Illinois/NCSA license and its retained third-party notices. Source URLs and
hashes refer to the exact version files used for this notice collection.
